Featured project

Penholt

IB Student Workspace · Full-stack SaaS · Founder

penholt.com

Penholt is a workspace built specifically for IB Diploma students — a single platform to plan, draft, and submit Internal Assessments, the Extended Essay, and TOK work. I identified a gap in how IB students manage their two-year workload: existing tools are either school-administered (Managebac) or generic (Notion). Penholt is the first student-side tool built around IB assessment criteria and deadlines.

IA Workspace
Structured editor for Math, Sciences, and Humanities IAs — aligned to IB criterion rubrics, with word count and section tracking.
Extended Essay Planner
Chapter-by-chapter EE builder with supervisor comment flow, draft versioning, and 4,000-word guardrails.
TOK & CAS Tracker
Exhibition object planner, TOK essay argument builder, and CAS hour log across Creativity, Activity, and Service.
Deadline Dashboard
Personalised study planner that maps IA deadlines, EE submissions, and exam timetables into a single view.

Built and deployed independently — React frontend on Cloudflare Pages, Supabase for auth and database, Resend for transactional email, OTP-based authentication, custom domain and DNS setup, Google Search Console indexing, and sitemap configuration. Full ownership from product scoping to live deployment.
